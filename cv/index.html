Professional Experience

  • June 2023–present
    Machine Learning Postdoctoral Research Fellow
    Scientific Data Division, Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory
    • Develop reinforcement learning for on-chip packet routing in NIFTY using proximal policy optimization (PPO), a convolutional critic, and a multilayer perceptron actor shared across nodes.
    • Co-developed an AI agent system for high-energy physics data analysis, including analyses for ATLAS.
    • Develop ATLAS GNN4ITk, a graph neural network pipeline for particle track reconstruction, and contribute to its ACORN research framework.
    • Serve GNN4ITk through NVIDIA Triton Inference Server with Athena as the client. Benchmark inference latency with TensorRT, ONNX Runtime, and different numerical precisions, and measure throughput as concurrency, model instances, and GPU counts vary.
    • Developed D-WALK and Junction Resolution for graph segmentation and presented them at CHEP 2026. On ColliderML at mean pileup 200, D-WALK cut mean CPU segmentation time from 118 to 23 ms while retaining 98.6% track-finding efficiency.
    • Introduced Double Metric Learning for directed graph construction and proposed and developed EggNet, presented at the ICML 2024 AI for Science Workshop.
    • Co-developed a multitask graph neural network with a UC Berkeley URAP student to classify edges and predict track parameters. Continue to develop the architecture independently.
  • Sept 2022–May 2023
    Visiting Researcher
    Machine Learning Group, Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory
    • Developed Unbinned Profiled Unfolding. In a simulated Higgs-to-diphoton benchmark, it recovered the particle-level transverse momentum spectrum and fitted a photon resolution scale of 1.19 ± 0.007 for an injected value of 1.20.
    • Fitted a generative hadronization model to two Herwig configurations using final-state hadrons as fit targets and extended HadML to generate both kinematics and particle identities.
  • Sept 2021–Aug 2022
    Visiting Researcher
    ATLAS Group, Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory
    • Designed and ran electrical tests to validate ATLAS ITk pixel modules and readout chips.
    • Built a Python and C++ tool from scratch to automate pixel module quality control. Argonne, Berkeley Lab, and SLAC adopted the tool.
  • May 2018–Aug 2021
    PhD Researcher
    CERN, Geneva, Switzerland
    • Led four ATLAS analyses on Higgs boson physics and dark matter, producing four journal articles and four ATLAS conference notes. Delivered five analysis approval talks.
    • Improved signal sensitivity by over 200% with ML event categorization and recovered a Higgs boson signal using unsupervised anomaly detection at over 10 standard deviations.
  • July 2013–June 2017
    Research Assistant
    National Tsing Hua University
    • Developed a machine learning method to distinguish Higgs boson production modes.
    • Modeled dark matter distributions and their effects on neutrino oscillations, deriving a bound on dark matter–neutrino coupling twice as stringent as the previous bound.
    • Used molecular beam epitaxy to study a 214 p-wave superconductor.
